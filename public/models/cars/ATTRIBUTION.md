## Changes made by BEKIUZ DRIVE

The GLB files are loaded as third-party assets. BEKIUZ DRIVE normalizes their orientation and scale at runtime to fit the game vehicle rig; selected paint materials may also receive the player's chosen body colour. The upstream asset catalogue reports that its models were optimized (vertex welding, geometry simplification, pruning and meshopt compression) before publication.

The game code, physics and surrounding environment are separate from these third-party 3D models. No manufacturer endorsement is implied. Vehicle names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
